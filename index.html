Our Story

Clay, colour &
zero rules

Kwarki Naari started as a lockdown sketchbook and a block of clay. What began as a way to cope became a love language — pieces so personal, so colourful, they couldn't stay in a box.

Everything is made by hand. No moulds, no machines, no duplicates. We sculpt, paint with bright acrylics, and seal each piece with a crystal-clear resin that gives it that glossy, candy-like finish.

The Craft

From raw clay to
resin-glossed art

01
✏️
Sketch & Imagine

Every piece begins as a rough sketch — no templates, pure intuition. We design shapes that feel alive and wearable.

02
🧱
Sculpt & Bake

Polymer clay is hand-conditioned and sculpted with no moulds. Each shape is unique before it even hits the oven.

03
🎨
Paint & Layer

Vivid acrylics are applied in multiple layered coats — gradients, patterns, and colour combos that pop and sing.

04
✨
Resin & Sign

A crystal-clear epoxy resin seals everything in a high-gloss candy coat. Then it's signed, titled, and sent out to its person.
